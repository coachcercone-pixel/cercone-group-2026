Dylan's story

But reaching the top changed his definition of success.

Dylan Cercone knows what it means to build from nothing—and what it takes to start again.

Today, as Founder & President of Cercone Group, Dylan brings those lessons to the stage, the sideline, and the boardroom. Through speaking, coaching, and consulting, he helps leaders and teams strengthen communication, take ownership, and perform when people are counting on them.

His story isn’t simply about how far he’s come. It’s about what he does with the experience and who he helps move forward.

Read Dylan's story

An only child, raised by a single mother, with limited resources, he was still surrounded by champions going on to be a 2004 New York State Champion in high school hockey. Later experiencing homelessness in Queens and Brooklyn before working his way to Wall Street.

Dylan eventually stepped away from Wall Street and returned home to Buffalo. He taught special education, coaches football at an inner-city public high school, and built a food truck business. Different rooms. Different responsibilities. The same commitment to helping people move forward.

His path continued through medical-device territory management, orthopedic operating rooms, and entrepreneurship. Those experiences shaped more than his résumé. They shaped how he leads: set the standard, build trust, and follow through especially when the pressure is on.
