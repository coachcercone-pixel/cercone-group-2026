Questions

Before you buy.

Do I need any technical skill?

No. If you can copy, paste and edit, you can use the pack. Every prompt comes with a plain-English fill-in guide.

What exactly do I get?

The workbook edition: a 36-page PDF, 24 single-prompt PDFs and 2 workbooks. The prompt pack: a 36-page PDF, a plain-text companion and 24 single-prompt PDFs. The weekly plan: a 4-page PDF and a plain-text copy. Everything is a digital download; nothing ships.

How does checkout work?

Choose an edition and the product page opens over this page. You complete checkout on Gumroad, and your files are ready to download right after purchase.

What if a file is missing or broken?

Purchases are final for change of mind. If a file is missing, defective or materially not as described, email executiveprompts@cerconegroup.com from the address on your Gumroad receipt, and we'll replace the file or refund the purchase.

Is this financial, legal or accounting advice?

No. The prompts give you a first draft to review. They are not a substitute for a finance, legal or accounting advisor.
